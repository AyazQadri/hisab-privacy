Hisab Privacy Policy

Hisab (حساب) — Everyday tools for Pakistan · Effective 27 October 2026

In short: Hisab has no account, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you type into the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

Who we are

Hisab is published by Stormware Technologies (package name com.stormware.hisab). Contact: stormwaretechnologies@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The following is saved only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Your favorite and recently used tools, language and appearance settings.
  • Records you create in Committee (BC), Udhaar khata and Event budget, and the city you choose for prayer times.
  • The last fuel price and currency reference rates that were downloaded, so the app works offline.

Other calculator inputs are kept only while a screen is open. We cannot see or access any of this information. It is not backed up by the app and is deleted when you uninstall Hisab.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. Hisab has no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no advertising and no tracking.

Reference data requests

To show reference rates, the app requests public data from these services. The requests contain no information you entered.

  • OilPrices.pk — petrol and diesel prices (CC BY 4.0).
  • Frankfurter — HMRC monthly currency reference rates (Open Government Licence v3.0).

Like any website, these services receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Prayer times and Qibla direction are calculated on your phone. The app does not use your location.

Permissions

Hisab uses only the Internet permission, to download the reference rates above.

Children

Hisab is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If the app ever adds features that collect data (for example advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
